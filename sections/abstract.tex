\begin{abstract}
Fast, safe, and durable charging is essential for lithium-ion battery
applications, yet charging-protocol design must balance charging speed, thermal
stress, and degradation under operating constraints.
Physics-based electrochemical--thermal simulation reduces reliance on repeated
physical cycling, but evaluating many candidate protocols remains
computationally expensive.
Multiobjective Bayesian optimization (MOBO) is attractive in this setting
because it can approximate a Pareto set with a limited number of expensive
evaluations.
Its early search, however, is sensitive to the small initial design, when the
surrogate contains little task-specific information.
This paper proposes LLMBO-MO, a large language model (LLM)-guided extension of a
ParEGO-style decomposition-based MOBO backbone.
LLM-informed initialization converts qualitative battery knowledge into a
protocol-domain-valid and decision-space-diverse initial portfolio, while
weight-conditioned regional guidance uses the active decomposition direction
and optimization state to add region-informed restarts to early expected
improvement optimization.
The numerical BO search is always retained.
All selected protocols are evaluated by the battery simulator before they enter
the surrogate training set or Pareto archive, and path-dependent voltage and
temperature feasibility is determined by the simulator.
Under 56 simulator evaluations and five seeds, LLMBO-MO achieves a mean
final hypervolume of \ChenLLMBOMean\,$\pm$\,\ChenLLMBOStd\ on Chen2020,
compared with \ChenParEGOMean\,$\pm$\,\ChenParEGOStd\ for ParEGO, corresponding
to a \ChenFinalGain\ improvement.
A similar advantage is observed on Ecker2015.
Mechanism studies further show that battery-specific LLM information is most
useful during initialization, while regional guidance acts as a secondary
early-search refinement.
\end{abstract}
