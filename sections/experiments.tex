\section{Numerical Validation and Discussion}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Evaluation Setup and Comparison Protocol}

All quantitative results are simulation based. The SPMe formulation in
Section~\ref{sec:model} is evaluated with the Chen2020 and Ecker2015 parameter
sets. Every protocol is scored by charging time, peak temperature rise, and the
common degradation proxy $D_{\mathrm{chg}}$. Each run contains six
initialization evaluations and 50 BO evaluations, giving a fixed budget of 56
simulator calls. Both parameterizations use seeds 8409--8413.

Final hypervolume (HV) is the performance metric; higher is better. Within
each parameterization, every method uses the same transformed objectives and
the same benchmark-specific ideal/reference box. HV values are therefore
compared within, rather than across, parameterizations. Reported uncertainty
is the sample standard deviation over the five seeds.

ParEGO is the primary comparator because it shares the
scalarization--GP--EI backbone without language-model guidance~\cite{ref4}.
NSGA-II and surrogate-assisted evolutionary methods provide useful broader
context~\cite{ref5,refDISK,refPIMD}, but their available runs use different
population budgets and are not included in the fixed-budget table. Direct
hypervolume methods such as qEHVI and qNEHVI remain important targets for a
future matched study~\cite{refQEHVI,refQNEHVI}.

The comparison holds the simulator, objectives, random-seed range, number of
initial evaluations, BO budget, and HV definition fixed. The initialization
composition differs by design: LLMBO-MO uses the screened portfolio, whereas
ParEGO uses six random protocol-domain-valid points. The main results therefore
evaluate the complete configurations rather than isolate one coupling term.

LLMBO-MO attains the higher mean final HV on both parameterizations. On
Chen2020, the absolute mean difference is 0.0032, corresponding to 0.84\%,
with three paired wins. On Ecker2015, the common-scale difference is 0.0121,
corresponding to 2.18\%, with four paired wins. The sample dispersions are
similar for the two methods on Chen2020 and lower for LLMBO-MO on Ecker2015.
These results support a modest configuration-level advantage under the stated
budget; they do not by themselves identify which advisory touchpoint causes
the difference.

\subsection{Pareto Protocol and Trajectory Interpretation}

Figure~\ref{fig:pareto_profiles} links the objective-space envelope to
time-domain behavior. The left panel pools 730 evaluated Chen2020 protocols
from five complete runs for qualitative interpretation; pooling is not used
to compute Table~\ref{tab:fixed_budget}. Point A is the fastest marked protocol
and exhibits the largest temperature rise and degradation-proxy value. Point B
has the smallest proxy among the five, C lies near the displayed
time--temperature knee, and D--E trade longer charging time for lower
temperature rise.

The trajectory panel illustrates how those trade-offs arise. The selected
LLMBO-MO schedule uses a brief high-current interval and reaches the target SOC
earlier than the two displayed alternatives, with a larger temperature
excursion. The voltage trace remains below the model-specific operational
limit. These are simulated trajectories and should not be interpreted as
measured lifetime or protection-system performance.

\subsection{Component Study}

The Chen2020 component study separates numerical initialization, the screened
warm start, regional guidance, and their complete combination. The first three
variants use one matched five-seed batch; the complete variant uses a
five-seed follow-up with the same seed range and BO budget. The region-only
test uses uniform anchors and a posterior-covariance adjustment controlled by
a mean-absolute-shift budget $B_\delta$; this component-only operator is not
the formal coupling in \eqref{eq:bounded_coupling}.

Warm starting gives the larger and more consistent individual change: mean HV
rises by 0.0066 with four paired wins. Regional guidance raises mean HV by
0.0026, also with four paired wins but with greater dispersion. The complete
configuration has the highest mean, while its 3/5 win count indicates
seed-dependent complementarity rather than uniform synergy.

\subsection{Limitations and Deployment Context}

The evidence is limited to simulation, two parameter sets, five seeds, a
trajectory-averaged degradation proxy, and a ParEGO-centered fixed-budget
comparison. It does not establish calibrated lifetime improvement or hardware
performance. Broader validation should use identical initial databases where
the research question requires a pure acquisition comparison, matched
qEHVI/qNEHVI budgets, model-discrepancy tests, and controlled physical replay
on the platform in Fig.~\ref{fig:experiment_platform}.
