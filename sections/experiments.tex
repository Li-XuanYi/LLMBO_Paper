\section{Numerical Validation and Discussion}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Experimental Setup}

The numerical study examines two questions: whether LLMBO-MO improves
fixed-budget multiobjective charging design, and which of the two LLM-guided
search mechanisms accounts for the observed improvement.
The SPMe-based simulator in Section~\ref{sec:model_section} is evaluated with
the Chen2020 and Ecker2015 parameterizations~\cite{ref8,ref9}.
Chen2020 is the primary case for algorithm comparison and mechanism studies;
Ecker2015 provides an additional parameterization for checking whether the
observed behavior is confined to one battery model.
Each candidate protocol is evaluated in terms of charging time $t_c$, peak
temperature rise $\Delta T_p$, and degradation proxy $D_{\mathrm{chg}}$.

The main comparisons use six initialization evaluations followed by 50
sequential BO evaluations, giving a total budget of 56 simulator calls.
For each battery parameterization, the same five seeds
(\MainSeedSet) are used for every compared method.
Hypervolume (HV) is the principal multiobjective performance metric because it
jointly reflects convergence and coverage of the nondominated set; higher HV
indicates a better solution set under a common reference convention.
Within each parameterization, all methods use the same objective definitions,
HV convention, and simulator budget.
The battery and LLMBO-MO settings are summarized in
Tables~\ref{tab:battery_settings} and~\ref{tab:algorithm_settings}, respectively.
The listed cell specifications apply to the primary Chen2020 case; Ecker2015
uses its source parameterization. Reproducibility details are provided in
Appendix~\ref{app:reproducibility}.

ParEGO is the primary BO comparator because it is a standard
scalarization-based method for expensive multiobjective optimization and shares
the decomposition--GP structure used by the numerical backbone of
LLMBO-MO~\cite{ref4}.
DISK and PIMD provide recent surrogate-assisted references for expensive
multiobjective optimization~\cite{refDISK,refPIMD}, while NSGA-II is included as
a classical evolutionary multiobjective baseline~\cite{ref5}.
Together, these methods provide both backbone-matched and complementary
multiobjective references.

LLMBO-MO attains the highest mean final HV in the Chen2020 comparison.
After 56 simulator evaluations, its five-seed mean is
\ChenLLMBOMean\,$\pm$\,\ChenLLMBOStd, compared with
\ChenParEGOMean\,$\pm$\,\ChenParEGOStd\ for ParEGO, corresponding to a
\ChenFinalGain\ relative improvement in mean final HV.
A paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test on the five matched seeds gives
$p=\ChenWilcoxonP$ (two-sided), the 95\% confidence interval for the
mean difference obtained from the reported mean and SD with the
$t(4)$ critical value is
$[\ChenCILow,\,\ChenCIHigh]$, and Cohen's $d_z$ lies between
\ChenDzLow{} (uncorrelated seeds) and \ChenDzHigh{} (correlated seeds).
Because the 95\% CI brackets zero under the conservative
seed-correlation assumption, the Chen2020 result is reported as a
\emph{descriptive} improvement: the direction is consistent across
seeds and across both parameterizations, but a five-seed test does
not by itself establish statistical significance.
The advantage emerges primarily during the early-to-middle portion
of the budget and is retained as the numerical GP--EI loop proceeds.
This behavior is consistent with the intended role of LLM guidance:
improving the allocation of scarce early simulator evaluations while
leaving objective evaluation, surrogate fitting, and Pareto updating
numerical.
LLMBO-MO also achieves higher five-seed mean final HV than NSGA-II,
DISK, and PIMD under the same evaluation budget.

\subsection{Effectiveness of the LLM-Guided Search Mechanisms}

\subsubsection{LLM-Informed Initialization}

The first mechanism study isolates the effect of battery-specific textual
knowledge during initialization.
A separate Chen2020 experiment uses ten matched seeds and a shorter
26-evaluation budget.
All variants share the same downstream numerical BO procedure and differ only
in the source of the initial candidate portfolio.

\begin{table}[!t]
\caption{Chen2020 Initialization Study over Ten Matched Seeds}
\label{tab:prompt_study}
\centering
\footnotesize
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4.0pt}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lc@{}}
\toprule
Initialization source & Final HV $\uparrow$ \\
\midrule
Random & 0.31062 $\pm$ 0.04303 \\
Detailed generic prompt & 0.31371 $\pm$ 0.04675 \\
Battery-specific LLM prompt & \textbf{0.36890 $\pm$ 0.00824} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Battery-specific LLM initialization improves mean final HV over random
initialization by \PromptHVGain.
The detailed generic prompt remains close to the random condition, whereas the
battery-specific prompt yields a higher mean with substantially lower
dispersion.
The paired comparison remains supported after Holm multiplicity correction
($p=\PromptHolmP$, $d_z=\PromptEffect$).
These results suggest that the observed gain is associated with
battery-specific prompt content rather than with prompting alone, supporting
the use of LLM-informed initialization in the data-scarce stage of MOBO.

\subsubsection{Bounded Regional Guidance}
\label{sec:component}

combination with LLM-informed initialization.
The short-budget Chen2020 ablation uses three matched seeds (8409--8411), six
initialization evaluations, and ten sequential BO evaluations, giving
\AblationTotalCalls\ simulator calls per seed.
All four variants share the same numerical multiobjective BO backbone.

LLM-informed initialization produces the dominant short-budget

(\AblationBaselineMean\ to \AblationRegionMean), and the three-seed
experiment does not establish an independent systematic benefit for
this component: a paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test on the three
matched seeds gives $p = 0.875$ (two-sided) for the
\emph{baseline} vs. \emph{region-only} comparison, $p = 0.125$ for
the \emph{baseline} vs. \emph{LLM-informed initialization}

\emph{complete LLMBO-MO} comparison.

adequate power at conventional $\alpha$ levels, these $p$ values
are reported for transparency rather than as evidence of
significance; the descriptive mean differences should be read
together with the dispersion columns of
Table~\ref{tab:ablation}.

variant achieves the highest observed mean HV (\AblationFullMean)
and the smallest observed dispersion ($\pm\AblationFullStd$) among
the four ablation variants.
This result is consistent with a complementary role for regional
guidance: LLM-informed initialization supplies the main global
improvement, while weight-conditioned region information can refine
the subsequent early acquisition search.

The representative protocols exhibit the expected physical trade-offs.
More aggressive current schedules shorten the charging horizon but
generally produce larger temperature excursions and higher
degradation-proxy values.
\emph{Protocol~A} (6.000/5.000/3.000~A, $\Delta s = 0.400/0.300/0.100$)
sits at the speed-favouring corner of the Pareto front: the first two
stages operate near the current upper bound, which drives
$t_c = 2880$~s but also produces the largest peak temperature rise
($\Delta T_p = 7.567$~K) and the largest degradation-proxy value
($D_{\mathrm{chg}} = 1.261$).
The $\Delta T_p$ rise is dominated by the high current in the first
two stages, whereas the $D_{\mathrm{chg}}$ rise is driven jointly by
the high current and the high average SOC at which this high current
is delivered.
\emph{Protocol~E} (2.000/2.000/2.000~A, $\Delta s = 0.245/0.153/0.403$)
sits at the durability-favouring corner: a constant current below the
3~A lower edge of the upper bracket, with the bulk of the SOC span
allocated to the third stage, gives $t_c = 7200$~s but reduces
$\Delta T_p$ to $1.528$~K and $D_{\mathrm{chg}}$ to $0.640$.
\emph{Protocols~B--D} trace the intermediate trade-off, in which the
current sequence is reshaped from the high-to-low staircase of A
toward the more uniform schedule of E; the deformation of the current
sequence is the dominant physical lever, whereas the SOC allocation
shifts play a secondary role in this regime.
Gentler schedules move toward the lower-temperature and
lower-degradation regions of the Pareto front at the cost of longer
charging time.
The time-domain trajectories in Fig.~\ref{fig:pareto_profiles} provide
the corresponding voltage, temperature, current, and SOC evolution
for these different operating compromises.

\subsection{Discussion}

The discussion interprets the numerical results in light of the four
design considerations stated in Section~\ref{sec:motivation}.

\subsubsection{Summary of the main findings}

The main Chen2020 and Ecker2015 comparisons both show that
LLMBO-MO achieves a higher five-seed mean final HV than the
backbone-matched ParEGO under the same 56-evaluation budget, and the
initialization study isolates the LLM contribution to the data-scarce
stage.
The advantage emerges in the early-to-middle portion of the budget
and is retained as the numerical GP--EI loop proceeds, which is
consistent with the intended role of LLM guidance: improving the
allocation of scarce early simulator evaluations while leaving
objective evaluation, surrogate fitting, and Pareto updating
numerical.
LLMBO-MO also achieves a higher five-seed mean final HV than NSGA-II,
DISK, and PIMD under the same evaluation budget.
In addition, a single-seed comparison across three LLM backends
(Section~\ref{sec:llm_backends}) shows that the LLM-informed
initialization gain and the subsequent BO improvement are retained
regardless of whether DeepSeek-V4-Flash, DeepSeek-V4-Pro, or
GLM-5.3-Flash supplies the candidates, indicating that the observed
benefit is not specific to one backend.

\subsubsection{Mechanism interpretation}

The 10-seed prompt study provides the strongest mechanism-level
evidence: the battery-specific LLM prompt raises mean final HV by
\PromptHVGain{} over random initialization and remains supported after
Holm multiplicity correction ($p=\PromptHolmP$, $d_z=\PromptEffect$),
whereas the detailed generic prompt remains close to the random
condition.
This pattern is consistent with the design rationale that the LLM
contribution is largest at initialization and depends on the
alignment between the prompt content and the battery domain.
The 3-seed component ablation is statistically inconclusive for the
regional-guidance branch alone, which is reported honestly in
Section~\ref{sec:experiments}.
The complete LLMBO-MO variant nevertheless achieves the highest
observed mean HV and the smallest observed dispersion among the four
short-budget variants, which is consistent with a complementary role
for regional guidance: LLM-informed initialization supplies the main
global improvement, while weight-conditioned region information can
refine the subsequent early acquisition search.

\subsubsection{Physical trade-offs in the representative protocols}

The representative protocols in
Table~\ref{tab:representative_protocols} exhibit three physically
distinct operating compromises.
\emph{Protocol~A} is the speed-favouring corner (high current, high
$\Delta T_p$, high $D_{\mathrm{chg}}$).
\emph{Protocol~E} is the durability-favouring corner (uniform low
current, low $\Delta T_p$, low $D_{\mathrm{chg}}$).
The intermediate protocols (B--D) trace a continuous deformation in
which the high-to-low staircase of A is progressively flattened
toward the uniform schedule of E, and the SOC allocation shifts
move the heat-generation load from the first stage to the third
stage.
The direction of this deformation is consistent with the LLM
initialization portfolio: the early-search pool contains
predominantly staircase-shaped protocols, and the GP--EI loop
explores the neighbourhood of these staircase anchors before
discovering the more uniform schedules that ultimately dominate
the durability-favouring region of the front.

\subsubsection{Scope and limitations of the conclusions}

The scope of the conclusions should be kept explicit.
First, all results are simulation based and the degradation
objective is a trajectory-averaged control-oriented proxy rather
than a measured capacity-fade endpoint.
Second, the five-seed main comparison establishes performance
relative to the selected comparator set; matched qEHVI/qNEHVI
experiments are not included and the results should not be
interpreted as an exhaustive state-of-the-art MOBO ranking.
Third, the regional-guidance ablation contains only three matched
seeds, so its independent contribution is less firmly established
than that of LLM-informed initialization and is positioned as a
secondary early-search refinement rather than as a peer
contribution.
Fourth, the single-seed comparison across LLM backends in
Section~\ref{sec:llm_backends} is descriptive only, and a
multi-seed, multi-prompt controlled comparison across LLM backends
remains outside the present study.
Two supporting numerical diagnostics---Pareto-archive growth and
objective-preprocessing sensitivity---are therefore retained in
Appendix~\ref{app:diagnostics}, while broader MOBO baselines,
larger cross-LLM studies, and physical-cell validation are left for
future work.
