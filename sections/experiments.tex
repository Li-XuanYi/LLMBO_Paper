\section{Experimental Evaluation}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Simulation Setup and Evaluation Protocol}

All quantitative results are obtained in simulation; no new physical-cell
measurements are reported. The simulator uses the SPMe formulation in
Section~\ref{sec:model} with either the Chen2020 or Ecker2015 parameter set.
The optimizer minimizes charging time, peak temperature rise, and the
protocol-level degradation indicator. Unless noted otherwise, one run contains
six initialization evaluations followed by 50 BO iterations, for a total of 56
simulator evaluations. Table~\ref{tab:eval_protocol} summarizes the two
fixed-budget comparisons used for the principal numerical claims.

The archive field \texttt{canonical\_hv} is the primary quality metric. It is
the hypervolume of the nondominated set in transformed objective space under a
fixed parameterization-specific reference box; higher is better. The value is
dimensionless but is not restricted to $[0,1]$, so absolute values are compared
only within the same cell parameterization. Reported uncertainties are sample
standard deviations across seeds.

The figures intentionally use three scopes. Fixed-budget tables report either
the Chen2020 seed-8409 case or five paired Ecker2015 runs. The Pareto-count
curve aggregates five archived runs per method, whereas the three-dimensional
Pareto plot pools Chen2020 LLMBO-MO archives to visualize the attainable design
envelope. Captions identify these scopes, and pooled archives are not used to
compute the fixed-budget statistics.

\subsection{Baselines and Comparison Scope}

ParEGO is the principal BO baseline because it uses the same scalarized
surrogate-and-acquisition structure without language-model guidance~\cite{ref4}.
NSGA-II provides an evolutionary reference~\cite{ref5}, while DISK and PIMD
provide surrogate-assisted evolutionary context~\cite{refDISK,refPIMD}. The
latter archives use their native population initializations and are not part of
the matched 56-evaluation statistical comparison. Direct hypervolume
acquisition methods such as qEHVI and qNEHVI are strong modern MOBO
alternatives~\cite{refQEHVI,refQNEHVI}, but no audited runs of these methods are
available in the present archive. Consequently, superiority claims are
restricted to the matched ParEGO comparisons in
Table~\ref{tab:fixed_budget}; all LLM suggestions are simulated before they
become objective observations.

\subsection{Ablation Analysis}

The four-group study isolates the two LLM touchpoints at the configuration

selected warm-start initialization. Figure~\ref{fig:ablation} therefore

\subsection{Runtime, Limitations, and Deployment Outlook}

The archived Chen2020 timing runs give $194.3\pm11.8$~s for NSGA-II,
$252.4\pm17.7$~s for ParEGO, and $440.3\pm31.3$~s for LLMBO-MO. LLMBO-MO is
approximately 1.74 times slower than ParEGO because wall time includes LLM
requests, response validation, region processing, and a larger acquisition
pool. Hardware and API-latency metadata were not retained, so these values
document archive-specific overhead rather than a portable runtime benchmark.

The evidence is limited to simulation, two parameterizations, at most five
seeds, and a ParEGO-centered matched comparison. The Chen2020 trajectory also
mixes a single-seed centerline with multi-seed contextual baselines, as stated
in its caption. Physical-cell measurements from the platform in
Fig.~\ref{fig:experiment_platform}, identical initial databases across every
baseline, and audited qEHVI/qNEHVI runs are needed before making hardware-level
or broad state-of-the-art claims.
