\section{Numerical Validation and Discussion}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Evaluation Setup}

All quantitative results are simulation-based.
The SPMe formulation in Section~\ref{sec:model} is evaluated with the Chen2020
and Ecker2015 parameter sets~\cite{ref8,ref9}.
Every protocol is scored by the charging time in Eq.~\eqref{eq:protocol}, the
peak temperature rise in Eq.~\eqref{eq:thermal_model}, and the degradation
proxy in Eq.~\eqref{eq:aging}.
Each run uses six initialization evaluations followed by 50 BO iterations,
giving a fixed budget of 56 simulator calls.
Both parameterizations use seeds 8409--8413; the prompt-design ablation in
Section~\ref{sec:prompt} extends this to ten seeds (8409--8418) under a shorter
26-evaluation protocol.

Final hypervolume (HV) is the performance metric; higher is better.
Within each parameterization both methods use the same ideal and reference
point so that HV values are comparable.
Reported uncertainty is the sample standard deviation over the five seeds;
for the ten-seed ablation, paired exact randomization $p$-values with Holm
correction are reported.

The shared-backbone design isolates the effect of the LLM advisory touchpoints
under a common scalarization--GP--EI pipeline.
All runs hold the simulator, objectives, seed range, evaluation budget, and HV
definition fixed.
Initialization composition is specified per experiment. Complete-configuration
comparisons use three screened LLM proposals and three random points for
LLMBO-MO versus six random points for ParEGO. The Chen2020 component study uses
pure six-point random or screened initial designs to isolate the advisory
touchpoints.

The primary Chen2020 component batch uses deepseek-v3, whereas the Ecker2015
comparison uses deepseek-v3-thinking. The preprocessing sensitivity study
states its separate deepseek-v4-flash setting explicitly. Within each matched
comparison, the backend and advisory interface are held fixed.

The experimental platform in Fig.~\ref{fig:experiment_platform} defines
the intended deployment interface for physical protocol replay.
All quantitative evidence reported in this section is simulation-based;
physical validation will use separately approved protection limits.

\subsection{Component Study}
\label{sec:component}

The component study isolates the contributions of the two LLM touchpoints on
Chen2020 under the same 56-evaluation budget and seed range.
Four variants are compared:

\noindent\textbf{Baseline} (Plain BO): six random initial points, standard EI
throughout, no LLM involvement.\\
\textbf{Warm Start}: six screened LLM proposals, followed by standard EI with
no regional guidance.\\
\textbf{Regional Guidance}: six random initial points, with bounded regional
guidance active during the early window ($T_{\mathrm L}=16$).
Because the warm-start screening pipeline is absent, this variant uses a
simplified anchor mechanism with uniform weighting and a mean-absolute-shift
budget $B_\delta$ in place of the full coupling in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:bounded_coupling}.\\
\textbf{Complete LLMBO-MO}: both touchpoints active (six screened LLM
initialization proposals plus the full bounded regional coupling during the
guidance window).

All four variants were executed in one matched five-seed batch using the
deepseek-v3 backend.

Table~\ref{tab:component} reports the mean and sample standard deviation over
five paired seeds. Warm Start attains the highest mean HV (0.3902) with a mean
per-seed gain of $+1.72\%$. Complete LLMBO-MO exhibits the lowest dispersion
($\pm 0.0067$) and the strongest single-seed gain, improving HV by $5.08\%$ on
seed 8413, although its mean gain ($+1.66\%$) is comparable to that of Warm
Start. Regional Guidance yields the smallest mean gain ($+0.67\%$) yet its best
per-seed gain reaches $+4.81\%$. The sizable gap between mean and best gains
across all variants indicates substantial seed-to-seed variability, so the
observations do not support an additive interpretation of the two components.

Figure~\ref{fig:component_diagnostics} complements the endpoint summary with
the full budget trajectory and the final mean gain over Baseline.
Warm Start and Complete LLMBO-MO establish an early advantage within the first
ten iterations, consistent with the value of informative initialization under
a small evaluation budget. Regional Guidance improves later in the budget but
retains a smaller mean endpoint effect.

From a practical perspective, these results offer a deployment recommendation:
the screened LLM warm-start alone provides most of the benefit at minimal
implementation cost, while regional guidance can be added for applications
where every marginal gain matters and an LLM query at each early iteration is
acceptable.

\subsection{Objective-Preprocessing Sensitivity}
\label{sec:objective_preprocessing}

This controlled Chen2020 study changes only the objective-preprocessing rule.
For each seed (8409--8413), all three variants share the same three screened
LLM proposals, three random initial points, scalarization weights, and plain-EI
settings; the LLM backend is \texttt{deepseek-v4-flash} with thinking disabled.
The compared denominators are the running objective range (min--max), the
historical objective standard deviation (z-score), and one (no normalization).

Figure~\ref{fig:objective_normalization} reports the first 50 cumulative
simulator evaluations. At evaluation 50, mean HV is
$0.3980\pm0.0328$ for min--max, $0.4087\pm0.0062$ for z-score, and
$0.3473\pm0.0597$ without normalization. Z-score has the highest observed mean
at this cutoff, whereas the unnormalized variant is lower and more variable.
These five-seed differences are descriptive.

\subsection{Prompt Design Ablation}
\label{sec:prompt}

\begin{table}[t]
\caption{Ten-Seed Prompt-Design Comparison (26 Evaluations, Chen2020)}
\label{tab:prompt}
\centering
\footnotesize
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{3.0pt}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lccc@{}}
\toprule
Warm-Start Prompt & Final HV $\uparrow$ & Improvement (\%) & Holm $p$ \\
\midrule
Random Init (no LLM) & 0.3106 $\pm$ 0.0430 & -- & -- \\
Experimental Prompt & \textbf{0.3689 $\pm$ 0.0082} & +18.8\% & 0.0059 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

To isolate the effect of prompt content, we compare a pure random
initialization against the experimental warm-start prompt under a 26-evaluation
budget over ten seeds (8409--8418).
As shown in Table~\ref{tab:prompt}, the experimental prompt raises the mean
final HV from 0.3106 to 0.3689, an 18.8\% improvement with paired Holm-adjusted
$p=0.0059$.
This result is the only statistically significant multi-seed comparison in the
present archive, and it underscores that prompt-content design is a
first-order factor in the overall system performance, separate from the choice
of the BO backbone.

\subsection{Scope and Next-Step Validation}

This controlled study evaluates the advisory interface under a 56-call
simulation budget on two SPMe parameterizations, using a common
trajectory-level degradation proxy and a shared-backbone ParEGO comparison.
The 17.8\% Ecker2015 mean gain and the 1.7\% Chen2020 mean component advantage
(up to 5.1\% in a paired seed) establish a positive configuration effect, while
the ten-seed prompt ablation ($p = 0.0059$) provides independent statistical
support for LLM-derived initialization.
The next validation stage will replay selected Pareto protocols on the
platform in Fig.~\ref{fig:experiment_platform} and extend the evaluation to
additional cell conditions and LLM backends.
