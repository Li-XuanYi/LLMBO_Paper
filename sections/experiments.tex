\section{Numerical Validation and Discussion}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Experimental Setup}

The numerical study examines two questions: whether LLMBO-MO improves
fixed-budget multiobjective charging design, and which of the two LLM-guided
search mechanisms accounts for the observed improvement.
The SPMe-based simulator in Section~\ref{sec:model_section} is evaluated with
the Chen2020 and Ecker2015 parameterizations~\cite{ref8,ref9}.
Chen2020 is the primary case for algorithm comparison and mechanism studies;
Ecker2015 provides an additional parameterization for checking whether the
observed behavior is confined to one battery model.
Each candidate protocol is evaluated in terms of charging time $t_c$, peak
temperature rise $\Delta T_p$, and degradation proxy $D_{\mathrm{chg}}$.

The main comparisons use six initialization evaluations followed by 50
sequential BO evaluations, giving a total budget of 56 simulator calls.
For each battery parameterization, the same five seeds
(\MainSeedSet) are used for every compared method.
Hypervolume (HV) is the principal multiobjective performance metric because it
jointly reflects convergence and coverage of the nondominated set; higher HV
indicates a better solution set under a common reference convention.
Within each parameterization, all methods use the same objective definitions,
HV convention, and simulator budget.
The battery and LLMBO-MO settings are summarized in
Tables~\ref{tab:battery_settings} and~\ref{tab:algorithm_settings}, respectively.
The listed cell specifications apply to the primary Chen2020 case; Ecker2015
uses its source parameterization. Reproducibility details are provided in
Appendix~\ref{app:reproducibility}.

ParEGO is the primary BO comparator because it is a standard
scalarization-based method for expensive multiobjective optimization and shares
the decomposition--GP structure used by the numerical backbone of
LLMBO-MO~\cite{ref4}.
DISK and PIMD provide recent surrogate-assisted references for expensive
multiobjective optimization~\cite{refDISK,refPIMD}, while NSGA-II is included as
a classical evolutionary multiobjective baseline~\cite{ref5}.
Together, these methods provide both backbone-matched and complementary
multiobjective references.

\subsection{Effectiveness of the LLM-Guided Search Mechanisms}

\subsubsection{LLM-Informed Initialization}

The first mechanism study isolates the effect of battery-specific textual
knowledge during initialization.
A separate Chen2020 experiment uses ten matched seeds and a shorter
26-evaluation budget.
All variants share the same downstream numerical BO procedure and differ only
in the source of the initial candidate portfolio.

\begin{table}[!t]
\caption{Chen2020 Initialization Study over Ten Matched Seeds}
\label{tab:prompt_study}
\centering
\footnotesize
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4.0pt}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lc@{}}
\toprule
Initialization source & Final HV $\uparrow$ \\
\midrule
Random & 0.31062 $\pm$ 0.04303 \\
Detailed generic prompt & 0.31371 $\pm$ 0.04675 \\
Battery-specific LLM prompt & \textbf{0.36890 $\pm$ 0.00824} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Battery-specific LLM initialization improves mean final HV over random
initialization by \PromptHVGain.
The detailed generic prompt remains close to the random condition, whereas the
battery-specific prompt yields a higher mean with substantially lower
dispersion.
The paired comparison remains supported after Holm multiplicity correction
($p=\PromptHolmP$, $d_z=\PromptEffect$).
These results suggest that the observed gain is associated with
battery-specific prompt content rather than with prompting alone, supporting
the use of LLM-informed initialization in the data-scarce stage of MOBO.

\subsubsection{Bounded Regional Guidance}
\label{sec:component}

The second mechanism study evaluates regional guidance both alone and in
combination with LLM-informed initialization.
The short-budget Chen2020 ablation uses three matched seeds (8409--8411), six
initialization evaluations, and ten sequential BO evaluations, giving
\AblationTotalCalls\ simulator calls per seed.
All four variants share the same numerical multiobjective BO backbone.

LLM-informed initialization produces the dominant short-budget improvement,
raising mean HV from \AblationBaselineMean\ to \AblationWarmMean.
Regional guidance alone changes the mean only modestly
(\AblationBaselineMean\ to \AblationRegionMean), and the three-seed experiment

observed dispersion ($\pm\AblationFullStd$) among the four ablation variants.
This result is consistent with a complementary role for regional guidance:
LLM-informed initialization supplies the main global improvement, while
weight-conditioned region information can refine the subsequent early
acquisition search.

The representative protocols exhibit the expected physical trade-offs.
More aggressive current schedules shorten the charging horizon but generally
produce larger temperature excursions and higher degradation-proxy values.

corresponding voltage, temperature, current, and SOC evolution for these
different operating compromises.

\subsection{Discussion}

Taken together, the main comparison, initialization study, and component
ablation support a consistent interpretation of LLMBO-MO.
The underlying optimizer remains a numerical decomposition-based MOBO method.
LLM-derived battery knowledge is concentrated in the stages where evaluated
task-specific data are most limited: initialization changes the distribution of
the first expensive evaluations, while regional guidance provides a bounded,
weight-dependent local search bias during early acquisition optimization.
After candidate selection, all objective evaluations and Pareto updates return
to the same simulator--scalarization--GP--EI loop.

The scope of the conclusions should also be kept explicit.
First, all results are simulation based and the degradation objective is a
trajectory-averaged control-oriented proxy rather than a measured capacity-fade
endpoint.
Second, the five-seed main comparison establishes performance relative to the
selected comparator set; matched qEHVI/qNEHVI experiments are not included and
the results should not be interpreted as an exhaustive state-of-the-art MOBO
ranking.
Third, the regional-guidance ablation contains only three matched seeds, so its
independent contribution is less firmly established than that of
LLM-informed initialization.
Finally, a controlled comparison across LLM backends and prompt realizations is
outside the present study.
Two supporting numerical diagnostics---Pareto-archive growth and
objective-preprocessing sensitivity---are therefore retained in
Appendix~\ref{app:diagnostics}, while broader MOBO baselines, cross-LLM studies,
and physical-cell validation are left for future work.
