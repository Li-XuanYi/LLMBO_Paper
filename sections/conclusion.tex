\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper addressed sample-limited multiobjective fast-charging design with
LLMBO-MO, a framework that uses language-model output as a guarded preference
prior rather than as measured objective data. A constraint-screened warm-start
portfolio improves the initial design, and an early posterior-covariance region
lift biases acquisition without altering GP training targets or predictive
variance. Under matched 56-simulation budgets, LLMBO-MO improves final
hypervolume over ParEGO by 9.2\% in the retained Chen2020 case and by 17.8\%
across five Ecker2015 seeds. The ablation archive further shows positive mean
contributions from both LLM touchpoints, with their combination attaining the
highest mean hypervolume.

The current evidence remains bounded by two simulated parameterizations, small
seed counts, a semi-empirical degradation indicator, and the absence of matched
qEHVI/qNEHVI and physical-cell results. The next step is to replay selected
Pareto protocols on the laboratory platform documented in
Fig.~\ref{fig:experiment_platform}, quantify measurement noise and cell-to-cell
variation, and evaluate stronger direct-hypervolume baselines under identical
initial databases. Extending the framework to variable-stage and mixed
discrete--continuous protocols, as well as locally hosted LLM backends, is also
of practical interest.
