\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presented LLMBO-MO, which uses LLM output as a screened preference
rather than measured objective data. Its two touchpoints construct a screened
warm-start portfolio and provide early weight-conditioned regional guidance,
while the simulator, GP, and EI retain authority over the evaluated database.
Accepted preferences provide external acquisition restarts and a bounded
posterior-covariance adjustment, with explicit recovery to plain EI.

With 56 simulator evaluations per run and seeds 8409--8413, LLMBO-MO attains
higher mean final hypervolume than ParEGO on both tested parameterizations:
0.3885 versus 0.3853 for Chen2020 and 0.5660 versus 0.5540 for Ecker2015.
The corresponding paired win counts are 3/5 and 4/5. The component study
identifies the protocol-domain-screened warm start as the more consistent
individual contributor and shows that bounded regional guidance can provide
an additional, but seed-dependent, improvement.

The present evidence is simulation based and uses a trajectory-averaged
degradation proxy. Future work will extend the comparison to direct
hypervolume BO, examine model discrepancy, and replay selected Pareto
protocols on the platform in Fig.~\ref{fig:experiment_platform} under
independently defined protection limits.
